Detection + tracking settings

Phone detection uses Xenova/yolos-tiny through Transformers.js. GPS uses your phone's browser location permission. Camera frames are processed on the device and are not saved by this app.

What gets recorded?

The route, timestamps, available GPS speed/heading/accuracy, total distance, drive duration, distraction starts, trigger changes, visible-phone detections, spoken warnings, alarms, recoveries and the location of each event when GPS is available.

Experimental driver-assistance prototype — not a certified safety system and not a substitute for attentive driving. Browser backgrounding, battery restrictions, GPS reception and permissions can interrupt tracking.
