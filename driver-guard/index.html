What counts as a distraction?

Head turned away, looking down/up beyond your calibrated forward pose, prolonged eye closure, your face leaving the camera, or a visible cell phone. A short distraction triggers a voice warning; continuing triggers a repeating siren and vibration.

Experimental driver-assistance prototype — not a certified safety system and not a substitute for attentive driving.
