Detection + tracking settings

v6 behavior: eyes-closed alerts are intentionally faster than the general sliders. The face detector reacts immediately on-screen, uses accumulated evidence so a single missed frame does not reset everything, and slowly adapts its forward-pose baseline while you are attentive.

AI models: face/eye/head landmarks are loaded from Hugging Face using face_landmarker.task; phone detection uses Xenova/yolos-tiny through Hugging Face Transformers.js.

What should trigger?

Sustained closed eyes, head turned away, looking significantly up/down, your face leaving the camera, or a visible phone. Normal quick blinks are filtered. The live status now shows eye/turn evidence before the full trigger fires, then gives a spoken warning and a repeating alarm if the condition continues. Every trigger is logged and pinned to the map when GPS is available.

Experimental driver-assistance prototype — not a certified safety system and not a substitute for attentive driving. Test detection while parked before relying on it.
